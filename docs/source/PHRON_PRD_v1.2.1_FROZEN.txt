1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

1.1 What PHRON Is
PHRON is a web-based, AI-native personal finance mentor engineered for young, salaried Indian professionals. It transforms personal finance from passive, guilt-inducing bookkeeping into a closed-loop system of practical spending judgment. PHRON combines a deterministic reconciled financial ledger with an empathetic, non-judgmental AI mentor persona to guide users through daily spending, overspend recovery, and milestone goal achievement.

1.2 Who It Serves
The initial operational cohort (ICP v0) consists of salaried urban professionals in India (ages 22-32) earning Rs 35,000 to Rs 1,50,000 per month. These users manage non-negotiable obligations (rent, EMIs, utilities), spend via frictionless cashless channels (UPI, debit cards, quick-commerce), desire concrete milestone purchases (electronics sales, travel), and have repeatedly abandoned manual spreadsheets due to administrative burnout.

1.3 What Problem It Solves
Cashless payment rails have decoupled the emotional weight of paying from the act of purchasing, resulting in the "Month-End Shock"—discretionary cash vanishing into micro-transactions, stalling savings goals. Existing solutions either provide backward-looking charts without guidance, require tedious manual categorization, or dispense generic preaching ("faltu ka gyan") detached from the user's live paycheck reality.

1.4 The Architectural Core
PHRON rejects the commoditized "Expense Tracker + Chatbot" pattern. It implements a strict separation of concerns:
1. Deterministic Engine (TypeScript/PostgreSQL): Governs all balances, dual-bounded Safe-to-Spend limits, bucket decrements, cross-tab spending matrices, goal trajectory run-rates, and next-cycle commitment ledgers.
2. Stateless LLM Extraction Utility (Gemini/Groq): Ingests messy, natural-language Hinglish spend inputs (e.g., "450 zomato and 2k clothes") and converts them into structured JSON records. The LLM never sees total bank balances, salaries, or net debt figures.
3. Empathetic Coach Interface: Formulates non-punitive, mathematically sound recovery plans when spending breaches predefined thresholds, ensuring users stay engaged rather than abandoning the tool due to shame.

4. PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY & BEHAVIORAL MODEL
================================================================================

- Practical Judgment Over Academic Theory: Financial stability is forged through contextual, daily spending micro-decisions, not by memorizing financial textbooks or analyzing abstract charts.
- Agency Over Automation: Users must author their bucket limits, approve recovery plans, and confirm reconciliations. Total automation creates passive disengagement; PHRON builds conscious financial judgment.
- Empathy Without Deception: Setbacks are normalized ("it happens!"), but mathematical realities are never obscured. Deficits are stated transparently and paired immediately with an actionable recovery path.
- Recovery Over Disciplinary Shame: Budget overruns are treated as operational deltas requiring mathematical rebalancing, never as moral failures. Alarmist red text and shaming notifications are strictly banned.
- Visibility Without Bookkeeping Burden: Natural-language shorthand logging eliminates the friction of forms, dropdowns, and manual spreadsheets.

5. PROBLEM DEFINITION & EMPIRICAL EVIDENCE BASE
================================================================================

5.1 Core Problem
Young, salaried urban professionals in India experience chronic financial leakage. Frictionless cashless payment rails (UPI, debit cards, BNPL, credit cards) detach the emotional weight of paying from consumption. Discretionary capital vanishes during the first half of the pay cycle, producing month-end shock, eliminating savings velocity, and triggering cycle-to-cycle anxiety.

5.2 Evidence & Observation Base
- EV-01 [CONFIRMED EVIDENCE]: Founder personal experience (n=1) and Friend B testing confirm that manual expense logging via spreadsheets or multi-step mobile apps is abandoned within 7 to 14 days due to cognitive fatigue.
- EV-02 [CONFIRMED EVIDENCE]: Automated mutual fund SIPs succeed because they execute on Day 1 without cognitive intervention, whereas discretionary budgeting fails because it requires continuous manual intervention.
- EV-03 [CONFIRMED EVIDENCE]: Fixed-date milestone purchases (e.g., festive electronics sales) fail because users cannot back-calculate the required weekly category cuts or recover from interim overspending.
- EV-04 [CONFIRMED DECISION]: During discovery testing (Q15), the founder explicitly rejected blunt, loss-framed messaging ("You have blown your budget") in favor of an empathetic coach persona ("Rough week, but it happens! Here is the plan").
- EV-05 [CONFIRMED EVIDENCE]: Quick-commerce platforms (Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart) blend essential groceries with high-margin impulse snacks, causing frequent categorization ambiguity.
- EV-06 [CONFIRMED EVIDENCE]: Individuals with debt obligations exceeding total income (Friend A archetype) cannot use standard budgeting tools and require a dedicated Financial Distress mode.

9. PRODUCT PRINCIPLES
================================================================================

- PP-01: Practical Wisdom Over Academic Advice. Every metric, card, and message must inform a concrete action today. Theoretical finance advice is banned.
- PP-02: Deterministic Financial Core. Balances, limits, caps, and recovery allocations are computed purely in deterministic code. The LLM is strictly prohibited from doing arithmetic.
- PP-03: User Agency & Consent. The system proposes; the user disposes. The system never silently creates buckets, reallocates funds, or commits future budget capacity without explicit user approval.
- PP-04: Non-Judgmental Transparency. Bad news is delivered with calm clarity and paired immediately with an actionable recovery path. Moralizing or alarmist language is prohibited.
- PP-05: Ironclad Financial Safety Boundaries. PHRON will never recommend specific stocks, mutual funds, credit products, loans, or BNPL services.
- PP-06: Zero-Interrogation Ingestion. Capture must complete in under 5 seconds. Modal clarification dialogues are prohibited unless an input has zero decipherable context.
- PP-07: Privacy Isolation Firewall. Complete financial profiles (salary, total bank balance, total debt) must never cross to external LLMs. LLMs act purely as stateless text extraction utilities.

12. THE CORE CLOSED LOOP (AI-NATIVE BEHAVIORAL ENGINE)
================================================================================

PHRON operationalizes Aristotle's practical wisdom through an unbroken, closed-loop behavioral engine:
1. Financial State Ingestion: User provides raw natural-language spend ("450 zomato") or weekly balance snapshot.
2. Deterministic State Processing: System validates syntax, parses line items via stateless LLM or regex lookup, decrements discretionary buckets, and recalculates live Daily Safe-to-Spend.
3. Event & Impact Detection: System detects whether the spend is within budget, breaches an allowance, or threatens an active milestone goal trajectory.
4. Deterministic Recovery Formulation: If a breach occurs, the deterministic engine computes exact weekly category cuts down to protected floors and evaluates next-cycle discretionary commitment capacity.
5. Contextual Mentor Framing: Stateless LLM receives purely the computed deltas and translates them into an empathetic, forward-looking coaching message.
6. User Decision & Action: User reviews the plan, accepts or adjusts trims with one tap, or triggers an alternative allocation.
7. System Adaptation & Memory: System logs user preference, updates merchant category memory, stores next-cycle commitments, and recalibrates starting allowances for upcoming cycles.

17. CANONICAL FINANCIAL ACCOUNTING MODEL & INVARIANTS
================================================================================

17.1 Unified Dual-Vector Ledger Formulation
PHRON models financial state using two unified vectors: the Liquid Cash Ledger and the Budgetary Commitment Envelope.

1. Liquid Cash Ledger (Physical Cash Position):
   L_current = L_declared_opening + Inflows_confirmed - Outflows_logged + Drift_signed
   Where:
   - L_declared_opening: Verified bank balance at start of cycle.
   - Inflows_confirmed: Confirmed credited salary and positive balance adjustments.
   - Outflows_logged: Cumulative actual cash debits (paid commitments, cash/UPI/debit discretionary spends, tracked card liability payments, debt payoffs). Unpaid credit-card purchases are excluded.
   - Drift_signed: Net cumulative adjustments from Soft Detective true-ups (+ or -).

2. Budgetary Commitment Envelope (Allocated Permission Position):
   Available Discretionary Pool:
   P_disc = I_sustainable - (O_total + C_next_cycle + G_res)
   Where:
   - I_sustainable: Configured monthly net salary baseline.
   - O_total: Total mandatory monthly commitments (rent, EMIs, insurance).
   - C_next_cycle: Next-cycle discretionary budget commitment incurred from prior cycle overspend.
   - G_res: Virtual monthly savings allocations reserved for active milestone goals.

3. Live Safe-to-Spend Pool (Dual-Bounded Margin):
   Budgetary Margin: M_budget = P_disc - B_spent
   Liquid Cash Margin: M_liquid = L_current - (O_unpaid + G_res + CC_accrued)
   S_pool = max(0, min(M_budget, M_liquid))
   Where:
   - B_spent: Cumulative discretionary debits logged this cycle.
   - O_unpaid: Mandatory commitments remaining unpaid in the active cycle; excludes all tracked card liabilities, including those from prior cycles.
   - CC_accrued: Sum of non-deleted credit-card purchase amounts logged in PHRON across all pay cycles, less confirmed payments allocated to those purchases (integer paise, >= 0). Reserved separately from O_total/O_unpaid; initially zero when no tracked purchases exist.

17.2 Mathematical Non-Double-Counting Proof
- Liquid Cash reflects physical spendable money.
- Budgetary Envelope reflects allowable spending velocity.
- Mandatory Commitment Paid: L_current decreases by amount; O_unpaid decreases by amount. M_budget remains unchanged; M_liquid remains unchanged; S_pool remains unchanged.
- Cash/UPI/Debit Discretionary Spend Logged: L_current decreases by amount; B_spent increases by amount. Both M_budget and M_liquid decrease by amount; S_pool decreases by amount.
- Credit Card Spend Logged: B_spent += amount; CC_accrued += amount; L_current unchanged. M_budget and M_liquid each decrease by amount; S_pool is recomputed and cannot fall below zero.
- Tracked Card Liability Paid: L_current -= payment; CC_accrued -= payment; B_spent unchanged. For 0 < payment <= outstanding tracked liability, M_liquid is unchanged because (L_current - payment) - (O_unpaid + G_res + CC_accrued - payment) equals its pre-payment value. M_budget and S_pool remain unchanged. No part of this tracked liability also enters O_total/O_unpaid.

17.3 Core Financial Invariants
- FIT-01 (Conservation of Ledger Balance): L_current must strictly equal L_opening + Inflows_confirmed - Outflows_logged + Drift_signed.
- FIT-02 (Dual-Bounded Safe-to-Spend): S_pool can never exceed M_budget OR M_liquid: S_pool <= min(M_budget, M_liquid).
- FIT-03 (No Fictional Liquidity): S_pool cannot be positive if uncommitted liquid cash (L_current - O_unpaid - CC_accrued) is <= 0.
- FIT-04 (Floor Protection Invariant): Deterministic recovery cuts cannot reduce any bucket below its user-defined hard floor: B_balance_post >= B_floor.
- FIT-05 (Commitment Cap Absolute Bound): Next-cycle discretionary commitment cannot exceed 15.0% of projected net discretionary pool: C_next_cycle <= 0.150 * (I_sustainable - O_total).
- FIT-06 (Anti-Stacking Invariant): If a cycle begins with an active commitment (C_next_cycle > 0), additional next-cycle commitments are strictly locked.
- FIT-07 (Goal Reserve Exclusivity): Funds in G_res are quarantined virtually and strictly excluded from S_pool.
- FIT-08 (Mutation Invertibility): Editing or deleting transaction T must execute a mathematical reversal of its balance effects before applying new effects.
- FIT-09 (Signed Drift Reconciliation Truth): Post-reconciliation ledger balance must exactly equal user-reported bank balance: L_ledger_post = L_user_reported.
- FIT-10 (Distress Separation): STS = 0 while commitments are covered must classify as Budget Exhausted (State B), not Financial Distress (State C).
- FIT-11 (Cash Confirmation Precedence): Calendar date reaching salary day cannot mutate ledger balance without explicit user confirmation.
- FIT-12 (Credit Card Reserve & Non-Double-Counting): A tracked card purchase increases B_spent and CC_accrued equally without changing L_current; confirmed payment decreases L_current and CC_accrued equally without changing B_spent or M_liquid. CC_accrued equals outstanding tracked purchases across cycles, never becomes negative, and never overlaps O_total/O_unpaid. Verify partial/full payments, duplicate-payment rejection, rollover, mixed legacy/tracked bills, and correction reversibility (Sections 18.9 and 21.2).
- FIT-13 (Zero LLM Financial Mutation): All financial state variables mutate strictly via deterministic application logic; LLM cannot alter ledger state.
- FIT-14 (Integer Monetary Precision): All internal currency math executes using integer paise; floating-point rounding errors are prohibited.

18. DETERMINISTIC MATHEMATICAL ALGORITHMS & EQUATIONS
================================================================================

18.1 Canonical Safe-to-Spend (STS) Engine (Dual-Bounded)
Inputs:
- I_sustainable: Base monthly net salary (Paise, > 0)
- O_total: Total cycle commitments (Paise, >= 0)
- O_unpaid: Unpaid commitments remaining, excluding tracked card liabilities (Paise, >= 0)
- CC_accrued: Outstanding tracked card liability across all cycles (Paise, >= 0)
- C_next_cycle: Prior cycle commitment deducted (Paise, >= 0)
- G_res: Virtual monthly goal reserve (Paise, >= 0)
- B_spent: Discretionary spend logged this cycle (Paise, >= 0)
- L_current: Current declared liquid bank balance (Paise, >= 0)
- N_days_remaining: Calendar days remaining in pay cycle including today (Integer >= 1)

Formulas:
P_disc = I_sustainable - (O_total + C_next_cycle + G_res)
M_budget = P_disc - B_spent
M_liquid = L_current - (O_unpaid + G_res + CC_accrued)
S_pool = max(0, min(M_budget, M_liquid))
S_daily = max(0, floor(S_pool / N_days_remaining))
S_weekly = max(0, S_daily * min(7, N_days_remaining))

Worked Numerical Example (Healthy State):
- I_sustainable = Rs 75,000 (7,500,000 paise)
- O_total = Rs 28,000 (2,800,000 paise), O_unpaid = Rs 22,000 (rent unpaid)
- C_next_cycle = Rs 0, G_res = Rs 8,000 (800,000 paise), CC_accrued = Rs 0
- B_spent = Rs 14,000 (1,400,000 paise)
- L_current = Rs 53,000 (5,300,000 paise in bank)
- N_days_remaining = 18 days
Calculation:
P_disc = 75,000 - (28,000 + 0 + 8,000) = Rs 39,000
M_budget = 39,000 - 14,000 = Rs 25,000
M_liquid = 53,000 - (22,000 + 8,000 + 0) = Rs 23,000
S_pool = min(25,000, 23,000) = Rs 23,000 (bounded by actual liquid cash!)
S_daily = 23,000 / 18 = Rs 1,277.77/day
S_weekly = 1,277.77 * 7 = Rs 8,944.39/week

18.2 Goal Trajectory Run-Rate & Milestone Status
Inputs:
- A_target: Target purchase price (Paise, > 0)
- A_accumulated: Total savings accumulated to date (Paise, >= 0)
- D_total: Total goal duration in days (Integer > 0)
- D_remaining: Days remaining until target deadline (Integer >= 0)
- M_remaining: Pay cycles remaining (Integer >= 1)

Formulas:
R_monthly = (A_target - A_accumulated) / M_remaining
A_expected = A_target * (1 - (D_remaining / D_total))
Status:
If A_accumulated >= A_expected -> ON_TRACK
If A_accumulated < A_expected -> BEHIND
Pace_Deficit = max(0, A_expected - A_accumulated)

18.3 Discretionary Overspend Detection & Deficit Sizing
Inputs:
- B_alloc_i: Monthly allocation for bucket i
- B_spent_i: Total spend logged in bucket i
- Floor_i: User hard floor for bucket i

Formulas:
Delta_bucket = B_spent_i - B_alloc_i
Condition:
If Delta_bucket > 0 -> LEVEL 1 OVERSPEND DETECTED
Deficit_amount = Delta_bucket

18.4 Multi-Tier Catch-Up Recovery Engine
Inputs:
- Deficit_amount: Uncovered spend deficit (Paise, > 0)
- Available Discretionary Buckets: Array of { name, balance_remaining, floor, priority_rank }
Priority Ranks:
- Rank 1: Entertainment & Subscriptions
- Rank 2: Shopping & Personal
- Rank 3: Dining Out & Social
- Rank 4: Transit & Commute (Protected down to essential floor)
- Protected: Groceries & Household Essentials (Margin = 0)

Algorithm (Pseudocode):
1. For each bucket i in Tier 2:
   Capacity_i = max(0, balance_remaining_i - floor_i)
2. Sort buckets by priority_rank ascending (Rank 1 trimmed first).
3. Remaining_Deficit = Deficit_amount; Trims_Allocated = {}
4. For each bucket i in sorted list:
   If Remaining_Deficit <= 0: Break
   Cut_i = min(Capacity_i, Remaining_Deficit)
   Trims_Allocated[i] = Cut_i
   Remaining_Deficit = Remaining_Deficit - Cut_i
5. Output { Trims_Allocated, Uncovered_Deficit = Remaining_Deficit }

18.5 Next-Cycle Discretionary Commitment Ledger ("Future-Self Debt")
Inputs:
- Uncovered_Deficit: Deficit remaining after exhausting trims (Paise, > 0)
- I_sustainable: Monthly net salary baseline
- O_total: Monthly mandatory commitments
- Active_Commitment: Existing commitment carried from prior cycle (Paise)
- L_current: Declared liquid bank balance

Formulas:
P_disc_projected = I_sustainable - O_total
Commitment_Cap_max = floor(0.150 * P_disc_projected)
Available_Capacity = max(0, Commitment_Cap_max - Active_Commitment)

Condition:
If Active_Commitment > 0 -> PROHIBIT COMMITMENT (Anti-Stacking Invariant FIT-06)
Else If L_current < Uncovered_Deficit -> PROHIBIT COMMITMENT (No Fictional Liquidity Invariant FIT-03: User lacks real-world liquidity to execute spend today)
Else If Uncovered_Deficit <= Available_Capacity:
    Allow Next-Cycle Commitment: C_next_cycle_new = Uncovered_Deficit
Else:
    Cap Commitment: C_next_cycle_new = Available_Capacity
    Unresolvable_Shortfall = Uncovered_Deficit - Available_Capacity
    Trigger Reality Check Warning to User.

18.6 Three-State Health, Budget Exhaustion & Distress Engine
Inputs:
- S_pool: Calculated Safe-to-Spend pool
- O_unpaid: Mandatory commitments remaining unpaid, excluding tracked card liabilities
- CC_accrued: Outstanding tracked card liability across all cycles
- L_current: Declared liquid bank balance
- I_sustainable: Sustainable monthly salary baseline
- O_total: Total mandatory commitments

Formulas & Booleans:
structural_distress = (O_total >= I_sustainable)
liquidity_distress = (L_current < O_unpaid + CC_accrued)
budget_exhausted = (S_pool <= 0 AND NOT structural_distress AND NOT liquidity_distress)

State Evaluation:
If structural_distress OR liquidity_distress:
    CLASSIFICATION = STATE C: FINANCIAL DISTRESS / OBLIGATION RISK
    Actions: Set is_distress_mode = TRUE. Dining Out and Entertainment allocations set to Rs 0. Want-goals locked. Surplus routed to Debt Firefight sub-ledger.
Else If budget_exhausted:
    CLASSIFICATION = STATE B: BUDGET EXHAUSTED / RECOVERY REQUIRED
    Actions: Set is_distress_mode = FALSE. Safe-to-Spend = Rs 0.00. Recovery plan active. Mandatory commitments remain safely protected.
Else:
    CLASSIFICATION = STATE A: HEALTHY / NORMAL
    Actions: Set is_distress_mode = FALSE. Normal daily spending pace active.

18.7 Signed Balance Reconciliation & Soft Detective Drift Engine
Inputs:
- L_actual: Verified bank balance entered by user
- L_ledger: Calculated system ledger balance

Formulas:
signed_drift = L_actual - L_ledger
Drift_Threshold = 50,000 paise (Rs 500.00)

Workflow:
1. If abs(signed_drift) < Drift_Threshold:
   Auto-absorb into Unallocated Drift bucket silently.
2. If signed_drift <= -Drift_Threshold (Missing Money / Negative Drift):
   Display Tier-1 Recall Prompt: "Looks like there's an unrecorded Rs [X] gap. Any ballpark idea where it went?"
   User enters estimated amount A_recalled for Category C:
   Debit Category C by A_recalled; Debit Unallocated Drift by abs(signed_drift) - A_recalled.
3. If signed_drift >= +Drift_Threshold (Extra Money / Positive Drift):
   Display Positive Adjustment Prompt: "Looks like your balance is Rs [X] higher than tracked (refund, reimbursement, or deposit)."
   User confirms -> Positive reconciliation credit logged; L_ledger updated to match L_actual; Safe-to-Spend increases.

18.8 Mid-Cycle Onboarding & Declared State Initialization Engine
Inputs:
- D_onboard: Calendar day user joins (Integer 1-31)
- D_in_cycle: Total days in current pay cycle (Integer 28-31)
- L_declared: Actual current liquid bank balance entered by user
- O_unpaid: Declared remaining unpaid commitments before next salary
- I_sustainable: Stated regular monthly take-home salary

Formulas:
D_remaining = (D_in_cycle - D_onboard) + 1
P_disc_remaining = max(0, L_declared - O_unpaid)
S_daily_initial = max(0, floor(P_disc_remaining / D_remaining))
Starter Bucket Allocations:
B_alloc_initial_i = floor(P_disc_remaining * Baseline_Weight_i)

Worked Numerical Example:
- Rohan onboards on October 18 (D_onboard = 18, D_in_cycle = 31). D_remaining = 14 days.
- L_declared = Rs 28,000 (actual bank balance).
- O_unpaid = Rs 7,000 (utility bill and Wi-Fi unpaid; rent was already paid on Oct 1 offline).
- Full sustainable salary = Rs 65,000 (preserved for future cycle baseline).
Calculation:
P_disc_remaining = 28,000 - 7,000 = Rs 21,000 for remaining 14 days.
S_daily_initial = 21,000 / 14 = Rs 1,500.00/day.
Full salary is NOT artificially prorated; real cash state governs the active cycle.

18.9 Credit Card Liability & Non-Double-Counting Workflow
Specification:
1. When user spends using a credit card (e.g., "1500 zara on credit card"):
   - Shopping spent and B_spent increase by Rs 1,500; CC_accrued increases by Rs 1,500.
   - M_budget and M_liquid each decrease by Rs 1,500; recompute S_pool = max(0, min(M_budget, M_liquid)).
   - L_current is unchanged because cash has not left the bank. Reserve the full unpaid tracked amount immediately, regardless of statement date.
2. In Mandatory Commitments (O_total/O_unpaid), "Credit Card Bill" represents only pre-existing or otherwise untracked legacy liabilities, never purchases already reserved in CC_accrued, even after a pay-cycle rollover.
   - User instruction: "Include only card balances not already logged in PHRON in fixed commitments. Logged card purchases reserve cash automatically until you confirm payment."
   - Do not backfill an already-declared legacy obligation as a new tracked purchase while retaining the same obligation; reject that duplicate representation.
3. When the user confirms an actual payment against tracked purchases:
   - Allocate payment to outstanding tracked purchase records; require 0 < payment <= their unpaid amount.
   - Atomically apply L_current -= payment; CC_accrued -= payment; B_spent unchanged. Record the cash outflow once. M_budget, M_liquid, and S_pool are unchanged by settlement alone.
   - Partial payment leaves the unpaid remainder reserved. Repeated submission of the same payment ID has no additional effect; reject overpayment against tracked liability.
4. For a bill containing both legacy and tracked amounts, require explicit allocation: legacy payment follows the existing obligation-payment path, while tracked payment follows step 3. Debit total cash only once; release each corresponding reserve only once; neither payment creates a discretionary expense. Never add the full mixed bill as a new obligation.
5. CC_accrued persists across salary confirmation, cycle rollover, and bank-balance reconciliation. Resetting B_spent does not reset or reclassify unpaid tracked liability. A balance reconciliation alone is not confirmation of a card payment. No card integration, lending, or new credit capacity is introduced.

19. EXPLICIT STATE MACHINES
================================================================================

19.1 User Account Lifecycle State Machine
- States: ANONYMOUS, AUTHENTICATED_UNBOARDED, ACTIVE_HEALTHY, ACTIVE_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED, ACTIVE_DISTRESS, DELETED.
- Transitions:
  * ANONYMOUS -> AUTHENTICATED_UNBOARDED via Google OAuth Success.
  * AUTHENTICATED_UNBOARDED -> ACTIVE_HEALTHY via Step-1 Onboarding (STS > 0).
  * AUTHENTICATED_UNBOARDED -> ACTIVE_DISTRESS via Step-1 Onboarding (structural_distress OR liquidity_distress).
  * ACTIVE_HEALTHY -> ACTIVE_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED via Discretionary Overspend (STS = 0, obligations safe).
  * ACTIVE_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED -> ACTIVE_HEALTHY via Recovery Plan Executed OR Next-Cycle Roll.
  * ACTIVE_HEALTHY / ACTIVE_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED -> ACTIVE_DISTRESS via Liquid Cash < Unpaid Commitments.
  * ACTIVE_DISTRESS -> ACTIVE_HEALTHY via Confirmed Salary Credit (Cash >= Commitments AND Salary >= Commitments).
  * ANY -> DELETED via Settings Delete Confirmation (Hard cascade purge).

19.2 Onboarding Workflow State Machine
- States: STEP1_PENDING, STEP1_SUBMITTED, MILESTONES_LOCKED, MILESTONES_UNLOCKED, FULLY_ONBOARDED.
- Transitions:
  * STEP1_PENDING -> STEP1_SUBMITTED via Form Submission.
  * STEP1_SUBMITTED -> MILESTONES_LOCKED via Account Initialization.
  * MILESTONES_LOCKED -> MILESTONES_UNLOCKED via >= 3 Buckets Logged OR First Reconciliation Completed.
  * MILESTONES_UNLOCKED -> FULLY_ONBOARDED via First Milestone Goal Creation.

19.3 Financial Health, Budget Exhaustion & Distress State Machine
- States: STATE_A_HEALTHY, STATE_B_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED, STATE_C_DISTRESS.
- Transitions:
  * STATE_A_HEALTHY -> STATE_B_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED via STS <= 0 AND M_liquid >= 0.
  * STATE_B_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED -> STATE_A_HEALTHY via Recovery Trims Accepted.
  * STATE_A_HEALTHY / STATE_B_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED -> STATE_C_DISTRESS via M_liquid < 0 OR O_total >= I_sustainable.
  * STATE_C_DISTRESS -> STATE_A_HEALTHY via Confirmed Salary Credit with positive liquidity margin.

19.4 Transaction Mutation & Correction State Machine
- States: DRAFT_INGESTED, PARSED_PENDING_CONFIDENCE, COMMITTED_HIGH_CONF, COMMITTED_MEDIUM_CONF, REQUIRES_CLARIFICATION, EDITED, REVERSED_DELETED.
- Transitions:
  * DRAFT_INGESTED -> COMMITTED_HIGH_CONF via Confidence Score >= 0.85.
  * DRAFT_INGESTED -> COMMITTED_MEDIUM_CONF via Confidence Score 0.50-0.84 (Swap Chip Active 24h).
  * DRAFT_INGESTED -> REQUIRES_CLARIFICATION via Confidence Score < 0.50.
  * COMMITTED_MEDIUM_CONF -> COMMITTED_HIGH_CONF via User Taps Swap Chip OR 24h Elapses.
  * ANY_COMMITTED -> EDITED via Inline Amount/Category Change (Re-executes Ledgers).
  * ANY_COMMITTED -> REVERSED_DELETED via User Delete (6s Undo Window then Soft Delete).

19.5 Milestone Goal State Machine
- States: DRAFT, ON_TRACK, BEHIND, ACCELERATED, COMPLETED, CANCELLED.
- Transitions:
  * DRAFT -> ON_TRACK via Initial Monthly Reserve Set.
  * ON_TRACK -> BEHIND via Pace Deficit > 0.
  * BEHIND -> ON_TRACK via Surplus Contribution OR Timeline Extension.
  * ON_TRACK -> COMPLETED via Accumulated Savings >= Target Amount.
  * ANY -> CANCELLED via User Action (Quarantined funds returned to Discretionary Pool).

19.6 Recovery Plan & Trim Adjustment State Machine
- States: DETECTED, COMPUTED_PENDING_USER, ACCEPTED, USER_ADJUSTED, SHORTFALL_UNRESOLVED.
- Transitions:
  * DETECTED -> COMPUTED_PENDING_USER via Deterministic Trim Engine Execution.
  * COMPUTED_PENDING_USER -> ACCEPTED via User Clicks [Accept Plan].
  * COMPUTED_PENDING_USER -> USER_ADJUSTED via User Customizes Trims (Validates Floor Invariant).
  * COMPUTED_PENDING_USER -> SHORTFALL_UNRESOLVED via Trims & Commitment Cap Exhausted.

19.7 Next-Cycle Discretionary Commitment State Machine
- States: ZERO_COMMITMENT, PROPOSED, COMMITTED_ACTIVE, DEDUCTED_REPAID.
- Transitions:
  * ZERO_COMMITMENT -> PROPOSED via Fixed Goal Breach Recovery Evaluation.
  * PROPOSED -> COMMITTED_ACTIVE via User Confirmation (Recorded as Next-Cycle Commitment).
  * COMMITTED_ACTIVE -> DEDUCTED_REPAID via Next Salary Cash Confirmation Day-1 Auto-Deduction.
  * DEDUCTED_REPAID -> ZERO_COMMITMENT via Ledger Reset.

19.8 Signed Balance Reconciliation State Machine
- States: SYNCHRONIZED, STALE_INACTIVITY, RECALL_PROMPTED_NEG, ADJUSTMENT_PROMPTED_POS, DRIFT_ABSORBED.
- Transitions:
  * SYNCHRONIZED -> STALE_INACTIVITY via Elapsed Inactivity >= 5 Days.
  * STALE_INACTIVITY -> RECALL_PROMPTED_NEG via User Enters Bank Balance (signed_drift <= -Rs 500).
  * STALE_INACTIVITY -> ADJUSTMENT_PROMPTED_POS via User Enters Bank Balance (signed_drift >= +Rs 500).
  * RECALL_PROMPTED_NEG / ADJUSTMENT_PROMPTED_POS -> DRIFT_ABSORBED via User Action.
  * DRIFT_ABSORBED -> SYNCHRONIZED via Ledger Updated to match Bank Balance.

19.9 Salary Cycle & Cash Confirmation State Machine
- States: CYCLE_ACTIVE, CONFIRMATION_PENDING, CASH_CONFIRMED, ROLLOVER_EXECUTED.
- Transitions:
  * CYCLE_ACTIVE -> CONFIRMATION_PENDING via Calendar Date reaches expected salary day.
  * CONFIRMATION_PENDING -> CASH_CONFIRMED via User Clicks "Yes, salary received" (updates balance).
  * CASH_CONFIRMED -> ROLLOVER_EXECUTED via Auto-Deduct Commitments, Reset Spend, Reserve Goals.
  * ROLLOVER_EXECUTED -> CYCLE_ACTIVE via New STS Initialized.
- Card liability persistence: CC_accrued and its purchase/payment records survive every transition; resetting cycle spend never releases unpaid tracked reserves.

21. TRANSACTION LIFECYCLE & MUTATION SEMANTICS
================================================================================

21.1 Ingestion & Parsing Lifecycle
1. Raw Text Ingestion: User enters string in capture bar.
2. Sanitation: Client strips non-printable characters and scripts.
3. Local Memory Intercept: Checks local KV store (`merchant_memory`). If hit, assigns bucket directly with High Confidence.
4. LLM API Extraction: If unmapped, calls Gemini/Groq stateless endpoint.
5. Invariant Assertion: Asserts parsed amount > 0.
6. DB Transaction Commit: Inserts into `transactions`; increments `buckets.current_spent_paise`; applies the payment-method-specific ledger effects in Section 17.2 atomically; recalculates Safe-to-Spend.

21.2 Mutation Semantics (Edit/Correction)
- Amount Update: Delta_amount = New_Amount - Old_Amount. `buckets.current_spent_paise` adjusted by Delta_amount. Safe-to-Spend updated.
- Category Reallocation: Old bucket decremented by Old_Amount. New bucket incremented by New_Amount.
- Soft Deletion: Sets `is_deleted = TRUE`. `buckets.current_spent_paise` decremented by Old_Amount. Safe-to-Spend restored.
- Historical Cross-Cycle Edits: Edits to transactions from prior pay cycles are strictly PROHIBITED in MVP v1.2 to prevent cycle ledger corruption.
- Tracked Card Corrections: For unpaid purchases, amount edits apply the same delta to B_spent and CC_accrued without changing cash; category-only changes leave aggregate liability unchanged; deletion/undo reverses/reapplies those effects. For partially paid purchases, amount cannot fall below allocated payments. Reject deletion or conversion to a cash payment method until associated payment records have been corrected/reversed; no edit may invent a cash refund or make outstanding liability negative. Cash/card method corrections reverse the old method's effects before applying the new method atomically.
- Payment Corrections: Reversing an incorrectly recorded current-cycle tracked payment restores L_current and CC_accrued equally, leaving B_spent unchanged; an amount correction reverses the old settlement then applies the corrected one atomically, within outstanding limits. The historical cross-cycle edit prohibition also applies to payment records; it does not prohibit a new current-cycle payment of an older tracked purchase.

================================================================================
22. BUCKET ARCHITECTURE & LIFECYCLE
================================================================================

- Obligations (Tier 1): Fixed commitments. Non-trimmable.
- Discretionary (Tier 2): Allowance tanks. Drains as debits occur. Protected by hard floors.
- Milestones (Tier 3): Goal accumulation tanks. Fills as monthly reserves credit.
- Mid-Cycle Allocation Adjustments: Users may increase a bucket allocation only if unreserved Safe-to-Spend pool has equal capacity, or by reducing another discretionary bucket.

================================================================================
23. MILESTONE GOAL SYSTEM & ACCOUNTING SEMANTICS
================================================================================

- Virtual Accounting Reservation: Milestone goals do not require separate bank accounts. On Day 1 of each pay cycle, `goals.monthly_contribution_paise` is deducted from available discretionary pool and credited to `goals.current_accumulated_paise`.
- Fixed vs. Flexible Semantics:
  * Fixed: Hard sale date (e.g., Oct 10 Big Billion Days). Cannot extend deadline. Deficit triggers next-cycle commitment evaluation.
  * Flexible: Elastic milestone (e.g., motorcycle). Deficit triggers deadline extension; next cycle's budget remains protected.
- Goal Cancellation: If a user cancels an active goal, `current_accumulated_paise` funds are returned to the active discretionary pool in the current cycle.

34. PRODUCT & BEHAVIORAL METRICS FRAMEWORK
================================================================================

- Product North Star Metric: Weekly Active Disciplined Users (WADU)
  * Computable Definition: Count of distinct users who log >= 1 transaction OR complete a balance reconciliation check-in during a 7-day rolling window, AND maintain an active, unrejected recovery plan if overspent. (Users with delayed goals remain included if actively engaging with recovery).
- Sprint Validation Metric: 2-Week Habit Persistence Rate
  * Computable Definition: (Count of pilot users logging >= 3 separate days in Week 2 AND >= 3 separate days in Week 3) / (Total Onboarded Pilot Users). Target: >= 60.0%.
- System Health Guardrail: Unassisted Categorization Accuracy Rate
  * Computable Definition: 1.0 - (Total Category Swap Chips Clicked / Total Transactions Logged via NLP). Target: >= 85.0%.
- Next-Cycle Commitment Safety Guardrails:
  * Pilot Invocation Rate: % of pilot users invoking next-cycle commitments.
  * Consecutive-Cycle Attempt Rate: Tracked to assert 0% stacking.
  * Pilot Kill Trigger: If >= 2 pilot users enter Financial Distress (State C) after using next-cycle commitments, feature is permanently disabled.

- FIT-09 (Signed Drift Reconciliation Truth): Assert post-reconciliation ledger balance equals user-reported balance exactly: L_ledger_post = L_user_reported.

CC_accrued equals outstanding tracked purchases across cycles, never becomes negative, and never overlaps O_total/O_unpaid. Verify partial/full payments, duplicate-payment rejection, rollover, mixed legacy/tracked bills, and correction reversibility (Sections 18.9 and 21.2).
