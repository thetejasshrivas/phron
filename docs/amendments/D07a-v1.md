## Exact approved wording

All monetary results are integer paise. For an unfinished goal, the next required cycle contribution is ceil((target − accumulated) / remaining_cycles), capped at the remaining target. Expected-to-date savings is floor(target × elapsed_days / total_days), with elapsed days clamped to the goal duration; pace deficit is max(0, expected − accumulated). Once accumulated >= target, required contribution is zero and completion takes precedence over pace status. For percentage-based starter bucket allocations, floor each share and distribute leftover paise by descending fractional remainder, breaking ties in the fixed starter order Groceries, Dining, Shopping, Transit, Entertainment. Preserve existing explicit STS floors.
