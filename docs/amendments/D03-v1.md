## Exact approved wording

Track carried_commitment_at_cycle_start separately from commitments_created_this_cycle_for_next_cycle. If carried_commitment_at_cycle_start > 0, prohibit new next-cycle commitments for that entire cycle, even after the carried amount is deducted. Otherwise, available capacity is max(0, floor(15 × (I_sustainable − O_total) / 100) − commitments_created_this_cycle_for_next_cycle). AC-BORR-01’s ₹4,500 is same-cycle commitments: under a ₹6,000 cap, at most ₹1,500 more may be committed. Scenario 12’s ₹4,500 is carried from the prior cycle: no additional commitment is allowed. Revalidate the cumulative cap atomically on acceptance; retain the existing payment-capacity condition pending its separate review.
