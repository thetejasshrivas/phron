## Exact approved wording

Track `G_cycle`, contributions reserved during this cycle, separately from `G_held`, all accumulated goal money not yet released or spent. Contributions increase both without changing cash. Rollover resets `G_cycle`, but preserves `G_held`, including completed-but-unspent goals.

Calculate liquid margin as `L_current − O_unpaid − CC_accrued − G_held`.

Cancellation releases the goal’s held amount once: decrease `G_held` and credit that amount to the current discretionary budget. Preserve contribution history so the same contribution is not also refunded by removing its original budget deduction.

A confirmed cash purchase using goal reserves decreases cash and `G_held` equally, without a second discretionary debit. Completion alone does not release reserves or record a purchase. Every operation is atomic and idempotent.
