# G3-DF-01-v1 — Debt Firefight accounting semantics

Status: APPROVED by Tejas, 2026-10-10.

Approved exact terms:

1. **Accounting Sub-Ledger, Not Cash Quarantine:** Debt Firefight in State C is strictly an accounting tracker and target allocation, not a virtual cash quarantine (`G_res`). Physical liquid cash remains 100% available to pay mandatory obligations and overdue debts.
2. **Surplus Computation:** While in State C, any unspent allowance from remaining essential discretionary buckets or uncommitted cash above unpaid obligations is labeled as “Firefight Margin.”
3. **Payment Destination & Invariant Safety:** Actual debt payments debit `L_current` and reduce debt obligations without creating discretionary overspend, preserving FIT-01, FIT-03, and FIT-10.

Scope note: Firefight Margin is descriptive accounting, not additional `S_pool`, a new reserve, or permission to spend. Its essential-allowance and cash-above-unpaid components are exposed separately to avoid treating overlapping accounting bases as additive liquidity. Actual payment remains an explicit obligation payment through the existing atomic ordinary-user operation. This amendment does not resolve card-funded goal execution or insufficient goal funding; those remain OPEN under D07b.

Evidence: `tests/unit/domain/firefight.test.ts`, `tests/integration/ph18/obligations.mjs`, and the G3 test results.
