# D17-v1 — APPROVED initial-cycle budget

Founder approval, 2026-10-06 IST: “Approve D17-v1 exactly as proposed”.
Scope: frozen PRD mid-cycle onboarding and §18.1. Frozen source remains unchanged.

## Exact approved wording

For mid-cycle onboarding, persist P_open = max(0, L_declared − O_unpaid) as the initial cycle’s discretionary budget. Subsequent calculations in that initial cycle use P_disc = P_open plus only separately approved budget adjustments, and M_budget = P_disc − B_spent, where B_spent counts discretionary spending recorded since onboarding. Do not replace this budget with I_sustainable or credit salary merely because a spend is logged or payday arrives. Replace the initial-cycle basis only through an explicit confirmed-salary rollover under the approved cycle rules. This amendment does not define overdue pacing, delayed cycle boundaries, drift budget treatment, or new goal/commitment adjustments.

## Approved example and verification obligations

₹2,500 declared cash and ₹500 unpaid establish ₹2,000; a ₹100 cash spend leaves ₹1,900 in both margins when no other reserves exist.

PH-11 tests must prove persistence across spending and retries, independence from baseline salary, and no calendar-triggered cash credit. AC-ONB-01/02 and FIT-01/02 remain the relevant mappings. Approval is source authority, not evidence that implementation or tests have passed. D05-extension, D22 and goal/commitment adjustments remain separate.
