## Exact approved wording

Compute financial dates in Asia/Kolkata. Expected payday is min(configured anchor day, days in that month). Before the next expected payday, remaining days = date difference(next expected payday, today), counting today and excluding payday. Cycle-day indices are relative to the cycle start, not day-of-month. Scenario 6’s onboarding date is October 31, not October 30: October 31 to November 1 leaves one spending day; October 30 leaves two. Apply the same date convention to 28/29/30/31-day months and non-first anchors. Reaching expected payday never credits cash or rolls over the cycle without confirmation.
