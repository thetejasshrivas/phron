## Exact approved wording

Each reconciliation changes cash exactly once. Positive reconciliation adjustments are recorded only in Drift_signed, never also in Inflows_confirmed. For a negative gap, any recalled cash spending is recorded once in Outflows_logged; only the unexplained remainder is recorded as negative Drift_signed. The reconciliation record’s full signed_drift is audit metadata and must not be added again to the cash ledger. Require 0 <= recalled_amount <= absolute negative gap and a stable operation ID. After confirmation, L_current must equal the reported balance exactly. Reconciliation never implies a tracked card payment.
