## Exact approved terms

1. Strict Dual-Bound Precedence (FIT-02): A positive cash adjustment increases physical liquidity (M_liquid) and updates ledger cash (L_current). Safe-to-Spend (S_pool) increases if and only if M_liquid was the binding lower bound prior to the inflow (M_liquid < M_budget).
2. Budgetary Margin Ceiling: S_pool is capped at M_budget. When M_liquid >= M_budget, unallocated positive drift remains uncommitted savings cushion rather than inflating current discretionary spending permissions without a formal budget reallocation.
