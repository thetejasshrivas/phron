# D22-v1

Status: APPROVED; Tejas2026-10-08 IST.

## Exact approved wording

Unexplained negative drift reduces the active discretionary budget by its absolute residual amount once; positive drift changes cash only. Recalled discretionary spending is recorded once as spending, with only the unexplained remainder treated as a budget adjustment. Reclassification must reverse the prior residual budget adjustment before applying its final classification.
